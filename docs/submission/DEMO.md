# Show Ozymandias

Use the public demo at https://mihirduvedi.github.io/ozymandias/.
A desktop browser gives the sculpture and surrounding sands more room. Keep
sound off unless the recording can capture it clearly.

## Prepared video

[Tour highlights](tour-highlights.mp4) is a 70-second silent screen recording of

## Suggested walkthrough

1. Open the world at its entrance and choose Enter the poem.
2. Let the first lines reveal the standing legs and fallen face. Pause briefly
   at the face so the broken features and expression are visible.
3. Resume, then show the hand and the inscription. Read the boast as it appears
   in the stone and in the guided reading.
4. Continue to the last line and let the camera reach the wide desert view.
5. Leave the tour, select a plus, and choose another line in the poem to show
   the connection between the text and the world.
6. Show Controls, turn motion off, and open Text only. End on the public demo URL.

The uninterrupted guided reading takes less than 95 seconds along its tested
route. Allow about two minutes if you also demonstrate exploration and reading
controls. This is a suggested recording length, not an event-specific limit.
Check the signed-in submission form before choosing a final duration.

## Narration

Ozymandias is a poem you can walk through. The guided reading follows all fourteen
lines, from the traveler's arrival to the ruined statue and its inscription.

You can pause or leave the tour and explore the world yourself. The plus markers
connect a part of the ruin to its lines, and selecting a line takes you back to
that part of the scene.

The poem keeps its original words and line breaks. Keyboard controls, motion off,
and a text-only reader keep the reading available without the automatic tour.

At the end, the camera pulls back to the lone and level sands. The king's boast
remains in the stone, but the desert fills the view.
